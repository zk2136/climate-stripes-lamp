Let's verify that your micro:bit connects properly to your computer and can accept programs.

---

### Step 1: Connect Hardware
1. Take your **Micro-USB cable**.
2. Plug the small end into the top of the micro:bit.
3. Plug the standard USB end into your laptop.
4. The red/yellow power light on the back should turn on.

---

### Step 2: Open Microsoft MakeCode
1. In your browser, open **[makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org)**.
2. Click **New Project** and name it `Warming Stripes Test`.

---

### Step 3: Flash the test program
1. From the **Basic** category, drag a `show icon (Heart)` block into `on start`.
2. Pair your device: click the **`...`** button next to **Download**, select **Connect Device**, and follow the on-screen steps.
3. Click the purple **Download** button.
4. When the micro:bit finishes blinking, the 5x5 LED matrix will display a glowing heart icon.
