Welcome to the **Climate Warming Stripes Lamp** activity!

In this workshop, you will transform real-world scientific data into a physical, glowing piece of technology. Using a BBC micro:bit and a 12-LED programmable ring, you will build a physical model inspired by Professor Ed Hawkins' famous **Warming Stripes** visualisation.

---

### What You Will Build

Instead of looking at climate data on a flat computer screen, you will create a standalone ambient lamp. Each LED represents a decade of UK temperature history from 1904 to 2023, shifting from cool blues to warm reds as you move around the ring.

---

### What You Will Learn

* **Hardware & Circuits:** Connect an addressable RGB LED ring to a microcontroller without any soldering.
* **Working with Real Data:** Understand how historical climate anomalies are recorded, scaled, and processed.
* **Algorithms in MakeCode:** Use arrays, index loops, and conditional logic to translate numbers into colours.
* **Physical Prototyping:** Diffuse raw LED light using craft materials to create an ambient visual display.

---

### Equipment You Will Need

* 1x BBC micro:bit (v2)
* 1x Micro-USB cable
* 1x Kitronik ZIP Circle (12 RGB LEDs)
* 3x Alligator clip leads
* Tracing paper or baking paper (for diffusion)
* Craft housing (such as a small cardboard tube or paper cup)
